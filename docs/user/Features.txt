AC7MultiSP Features

1. Main features
1. All aircraft can sortie with up to three types of SP weapons.
   By default, each SP weapon has 50% ammunition when carrying two types, or 33% when carrying three. Both settings can be adjusted to 100%.
2. SP weapon mounting models have been made for most individual aircraft in the base game and DLC, displaying multiple SP weapon types on the aircraft at the same time to avoid abrupt model changes when switching weapons.
   This feature may reduce FPS or cause stuttering. It can be disabled during missions or in the hangar.
3. When carrying MGP/HGP/PLSL pods, the built-in gun and pods can fire together with one button.
4. Camera controls:
   • Optional persistent target tracking: press the target-tracking button to keep tracking without holding it down.
   • Optional camera hold: the camera does not automatically recenter after you move it with the right stick or mouse.
   • Optional VR-style target selection: when switching targets, enemies near the center of the camera view take priority as NEXT, instead of enemies in front of the aircraft's nose.
5. Debug features:
   • Change the SP weapon combination during a mission.
   • Unlimited ammunition and fast reload for SP weapons.
   • Change aircraft and restart the mission.
   • Quickly enter a mission from the main menu to help develop and test mods.

2. Common controls
These are the default controls. Keyboard bindings can be customized.
• F6 or controller Select + Y: open the management interface.
• Keyboard 1 / 2 / 3 / 4: select standard missiles / the first / second / third SP weapon.
• Controller D-pad left / up / right / down: select the same four weapons. Controller X still cycles weapons.
• Ctrl + Alt + F5: toggle linked gun fire.
• Ctrl + Alt + F6: toggle camera hold.
• Enable persistent target tracking separately on the Camera page of the management interface. Hold Y on the controller to start tracking, then hold Y again to stop. On the keyboard, press the target-tracking key assigned in the game settings once to start, and again to stop.
• Press L3: recenter the camera / stop target tracking. Hold R3: toggle camera hold.

3. Multi-SP weapon model support
1. Fully supported:
• F-4E, F-14A, F-14D, F-14A TGM, F-15C, F-15E, F-15J, F-15 S/MTD, F-16C, F-16XL, F/A-18E, F/A-18E TGM, F/A-18F, F/A-18F Block III, F-35C, F-2A
• Mirage 2000-5, Rafale M, Typhoon, Gripen E
• MiG-29A, MiG-31B (some combinations mount SAAM under the fuselage), MiG-35D, Su-30M2, Su-30SM, Su-33, Su-34, Su-35S, Su-37
• XFA-27, ADFX-01, ADF-01 FALKEN (weapons cannot be switched while firing TLS), ADF-11F, CFA-44 (ADMM on the left, EML on the right, ECM model hidden)

2. Partially supported:
• F-22A: the 8AAM pods are always visible. SP weapon models inside the fuselage weapon bays change when switching weapons.
• Standard Su-57: the PLSL pods are always visible. SP weapon models inside the fuselage weapon bays change when switching weapons.
• DarkStar: the PLSL pods are always visible. SP weapon models inside the fuselage weapon bays change when switching weapons.

3. Not yet supported:
A-10, ASF-X, F-2A Super Kai, F-104C, MiG-21bis
Su-47, Su-57 TGM, X-02S, FB-22, YF-23
Popular mod aircraft will also receive priority for future support.

4. Scope and compatibility with other mods
• For the Steam version, single-player campaign only. Disabled in multiplayer.
• Compatible mods: most add-on aircraft, skins, AC7MouseAim, Enhanced Gunplay, All Aircraft PSM, Modular Weapon Swap.
• Cannot coexist with: F-14IA.
• Can coexist with, but this mod does not take effect in: Pre-Campaign VR missions.

5. Known issues
• Linked gun fire is not yet compatible with some mod aircraft.
• The ventral weapon bays on some aircraft may remain open.
• The SP weapon performance bars do not change when selecting PARTs. This is a UI issue; the parts still take effect.
• Special effects triggered by holding the weapon-switch button on some mod aircraft may not be fully supported.
• Some aircraft with special sustained effects (such as APS on the ADF-11) cannot select them together with SP weapons.
• On lower-performance PCs, very low FPS while this mod is enabled may cause mouse-flight control problems. If this happens, disable multi-SP models to improve FPS.
