AC7MultiSP Features

I. Main features
1. All aircraft can sortie with up to three SP weapon types.
   By default, carrying two SP weapon types gives each type 50% ammunition, while carrying three gives each type 33%. Both can be adjusted to 100%.
2. SP weapon mounting models have been made for most aircraft in the base game and DLC. They display multiple SP weapon models on the aircraft at the same time, avoiding abrupt model changes when switching weapons.
   This feature may reduce FPS or cause stuttering. It can be disabled during missions or in the hangar.
3. When carrying MGP/HGP/PLSL pods, the aircraft gun and gun pods can fire together with one button.
4. Improved camera controls:
   • Optional persistent target tracking: press the target-tracking button to keep tracking without holding it down.
   • Optional camera hold: the camera does not automatically recenter after you move it with the right stick or mouse.
   • Optional VR-mode target selection: when switching targets, enemies near the center of the camera view are prioritized as NEXT, instead of enemies in the direction of the aircraft's nose.
5. Debug features:
   • Change the SP weapon combination immediately during a mission.
   • Unlimited ammunition and fast reload for special weapons.
   • Change aircraft during a mission and restart the mission.
   • Quickly enter a mission from the main menu to make mod development and testing easier.

II. Common controls
The following are the default controls. Keyboard bindings can be customized.
• F6 or controller Select + Y: open the management interface.
• Keyboard 1 / 2 / 3 / 4: select standard missiles / the first / second / third special weapon.
• Controller D-pad left / up / right / down: select the same four weapons. Controller X still cycles weapons.
• Ctrl + Alt + F5: toggle linked gun fire.
• Ctrl + Alt + F6: toggle camera hold.
• Persistent target tracking must be enabled separately on the Camera page of the management interface. Hold Y on the controller to start tracking, then hold Y again to cancel. On the keyboard, press the target-tracking key assigned in the game settings once to start, and again to stop.
• Press L3 once: recenter the camera / stop tracking. Hold R3: toggle camera hold.


III. Multi-SP weapon model support
1. Fully supported:
• F-4E, F-14A, F-14D, F-14A TGM, F-15C, F-15E, F-15J, F-15 S/MTD, F-16C, F-16XL, F/A-18E, F/A-18E TGM, F/A-18F, F/A-18F Block III, F-35C, F-2A
• Mirage 2000-5, Rafale M, Typhoon, Gripen E
• MiG-29A, MiG-31B (some combinations mount SAAM under the fuselage), MiG-35D, Su-30M2, Su-30SM, Su-33, Su-34, Su-35S, Su-37
• XFA-27, ADFX-01, ADF-01 FALKEN (weapons cannot be switched while firing TLS), ADF-11F, CFA-44 (ADMM on the left, EML on the right, ECM model hidden)

2. Partially supported:
• F-22A: the 8AAM pods are always visible. SP weapon models inside the ventral weapon bays change when switching weapons.
• Standard Su-57: the PLSL pods are always visible. SP weapon models inside the ventral weapon bays change when switching weapons.
• DarkStar: the PLSL pods are always visible. SP weapon models inside the ventral weapon bays change when switching weapons.

3. Not yet supported:
A-10, ASF-X, F-2A Super Kai, F-104C, MiG-21bis
Su-47, Su-57 TGM, X-02S, FB-22, YF-23
Popular mod aircraft will also receive priority for future support.

IV. Scope and compatibility with other mods
• For the Steam version of the game, single-player campaign only. Disabled in multiplayer.
• Compatible mods: most add-on aircraft, skins, AC7MouseAim mouse-flight controls, Enhanced Gunplay, All Aircraft PSM, Modular Weapon Swap.
• Cannot coexist with: F-14IA.
• Can coexist with, but this mod does not take effect in: Pre-Campaign VR missions.

V. Known issues
• Linked gun fire is not yet compatible with some mod aircraft.
• The ventral weapon bays on some aircraft may remain open.
• The SP weapon performance bars do not change when selecting PARTs. This is a UI issue; the parts do take effect.
• Special effects triggered by holding the weapon-switch button on some mod aircraft may not be fully supported.
• Some aircraft with special sustained effects (such as APS on the ADF-11) cannot select them together with SP weapons.
• On lower-performance PCs, very low FPS while this mod is enabled may cause mouse-flight control problems. If this happens, disable multi-SP models to improve FPS.
