AC7MultiSP

2.26.1 alpha3 (test build)
• Restored English for the specified fixed labels in native loadout and resupply menus. Weapon descriptions and takeoff prompts still follow the game language; F6 and the installer retain Chinese and English.
• Optimized CFA-44 EML muzzle checks during steady flight and reduced repeated camera, object-identity, rack and model-visibility checks. Actual frame-rate gains require in-game verification.
• Fixed the CFA-44 ventral bay: it stays closed with multi-SP models enabled. With models disabled, it opens only while ADMM is selected and closes after switching to EML, ECM or MSL.
• Unified compatibility-failure handling for sustained effects and weapon bays, isolating local failures by aircraft instance. Multi-SP operations remain blocked when cleanup safety cannot be verified.
• Tracked releases of temporary sustained-effect retention so cleanup failures prevent a successful response. Added switch-stage logs for other aircraft to help locate remaining stutter.
• Includes the UI transfer, loadout and steady-flight optimizations from alpha1 and alpha2.

2.26.1 alpha2 (test build)
• Reduced repeated model validation when selecting SP weapons in the hangar and resupply menu, plus model polling and data copying during steady flight.
• Avoided duplicate HUD refreshes after switching in the same update; immediate weapon switching, ammo and cooldown updates are retained.
• Added periodic performance breakdowns for comparing stutter after installation. In-game improvement remains to be verified.
• Includes the UI state-transfer optimization from alpha1.

2.26.1 alpha1 (test build)
• Optimized UI state transfer during flight and weapon switching by separating large aircraft and quick-mission lists.
• Management lists are transferred when F6 is open and an update is needed; weapon, HUD and operation-result updates remain immediate.
• Intended to investigate occasional mouse-flight camera recentering. Performance and compatibility still require in-game verification.

2.26.0
• Fixed weapon bays failing to close properly when switching SP weapons on CFA-50, standard Su-57, DarkStar and XFA-33 A/C.
• Added support for the S32's IRCM weapon, allowing it to be carried together with other SP weapons.
• Fixed freezing during mid-mission landing and resupply.

2.25.X
• Fixed the weapon-switch button's hold function not working, supporting ECM and other effects triggered by holding this button on some mod aircraft.
• Added startup logo skipping and quick missions. Select the mission, aircraft, difficulty, skin and 1–3 SP weapons to enter a mission quickly.
• Added an installer for one-click download and installation, while retaining Vortex compatibility.

2.24.X
• Added update checks and browser download links.

2.22.X–2.23.X
• Reworked the management interface.
• Improved controller camera controls.
• Added separate model switches for missions and the hangar, and expanded mounting visuals for more aircraft.
• Reduced the overhead of SP weapon switching and interface operations, improving stuttering when switching weapons on the CFA-44.
• Added unlimited ammunition and fast reload options for SP weapons, and expanded support for some third-party aircraft, drones, and gun-type SP weapons.

2.17.X–2.21.X
• Added persistent target tracking, camera hold, and camera recentering controls.
• Added linked fire for the built-in gun, gun pods, and pulse lasers.
• Improved SP weapon switching on aircraft including the CFA-44 and FALKEN.

2.6.X–2.16.X
• Added visuals for carrying multiple SP weapon types on more aircraft, with continued adjustments to mounting points and left/right layouts.
• Improved switching behavior for SP weapons such as drones and lasers, and added the ability to change aircraft and restart the mission.
• Restored the game's original model display for some aircraft with internal weapon bays to reduce stuttering, while retaining multi-SP weapon use.

2.0.X–2.5.X
• Renamed the mod to AC7MultiSP, supporting up to three SP weapon types on one aircraft.
• Added multi-SP weapon model display in the hangar, during resupply, and in missions, plus shortcuts for combinations and models.

1.X and earlier
• Named DualSP, with two SP weapon types, independent ammunition and reloads, and gradual improvements to the management interface and installation methods.
