AC7MultiSP

2.25.3 alpha4 (self-test build, unreleased)
• Corrected conflicting task and preview rack records that caused model previews to fall back while switching multi-SP resupply candidates. Unknown-resource checks remain in place; in-game validation is pending.
• Log session versions now follow the product version automatically instead of retaining the old 2.24.1 label.

2.25.3 alpha3 (self-test build, unreleased)
• Quick missions add skin selection while retaining 1–3 SP loadouts and existing presets. Skins are checked by actual number, DLC ownership and resource reference.
• Fixed a text-replacement error when displaying weapon descriptions on the resupply selection page, without changing ammunition or reload rules.
• Skin selection and resupply behavior await in-game validation. Repeated bay opening on XFA-33 B and Su-57 TGM remains deferred.

2.25.3 alpha2 (self-test build, unreleased)
• Quick missions support 1–3 selected SP weapons, save the complete test loadout and retain launch-time ammunition ratios. Existing single-SP presets remain compatible.
• Extended Backspace and Space isolation in F6 while paused, retaining text editing, rebinding and key-release handling after closing the panel. In-game validation is pending.
• Repeated bay opening when switching SP weapons on XFA-33 B and Su-57 TGM remains recorded and is deferred in this version.

2.25.3 alpha1 (self-test build, unreleased)
• Fixed weapon-name references for some mod aircraft, retaining author-provided names on aircraft such as F17V, ADF-11 and F-15EX.
• Added IRCM mixed-loadout support subject to native compatibility checks, retaining duration, ending and recycling behavior. Aircraft including S32 still require in-game validation.
• Added bay closing for CFA-50, standard Su-57, DarkStar and XFA-33 A/C. XFA-33 B and Su-57 TGM retain bay progress when switching SP weapons. In-game validation is pending.
• Aircraft lists now prefer in-game display names and support searches by CFA-50, internal code or ID.
• Extended Backspace isolation in F6 text fields to reduce accidental game pauses.
• The universal ZIP provides four documents in separate Chinese and English folders; English documents use English filenames.
• This build is for self-testing only. The official release remains 2.25.2.

2.25.X
• Fixed internal weapon switching interrupting native hold-button auxiliary effects on some mod aircraft.
• Added main-menu quick mission testing, separate test presets and optional automatic launch on startup.
• Added an installer for one-click download and installation, while retaining Vortex compatibility.

2.24.X
• Added update checks and browser download links, and refined in-game messages.

2.22.X–2.23.X
• Reworked the management interface.
• Improved controller camera controls.
• Added separate model switches for missions and the hangar, and expanded mounting visuals for more aircraft.
• Reduced the overhead of SP weapon switching and interface operations, improving stuttering when switching weapons on the CFA-44.
• Added unlimited ammunition and fast reload options for SP weapons, and expanded support for some third-party aircraft, drones, and gun-type SP weapons.

2.17.X–2.21.X
• Added persistent target tracking, camera hold, and camera recentering controls.
• Added linked fire for the built-in gun, gun pods, and pulse lasers.
• Improved SP weapon switching on aircraft including the CFA-44 and FALKEN.

2.6.X–2.16.X
• Added visuals for carrying multiple SP weapon types on more aircraft, with continued adjustments to mounting points and left/right layouts.
• Improved switching behavior for SP weapons such as drones and lasers, and added the ability to change aircraft and restart the mission.
• Restored the game's original model display for some aircraft with internal weapon bays to reduce stuttering, while retaining multi-SP weapon use.

2.0.X–2.5.X
• Renamed the mod to AC7MultiSP, supporting up to three SP weapon types on one aircraft.
• Added multi-SP weapon model display in the hangar, during resupply, and in missions, plus shortcuts for combinations and models.

1.X and earlier
• Named DualSP, with two SP weapon types, independent ammunition and reloads, and gradual improvements to the management interface and installation methods.
