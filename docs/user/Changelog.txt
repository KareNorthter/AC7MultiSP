AC7MultiSP

2.26.0
Major changes from the 2.25 series through this release.

Quick missions
• Added main-menu quick missions with mission, aircraft, difficulty, skin and 1–3 SP selections, separate saved presets and optional automatic launch on the next startup. Existing presets remain compatible; M13 retains its mission-specific weapon.
• Added an option to skip startup logos and moved quick missions above the log section on the Debug page.
• Fixed multi-SP initialization stopping after a quick launch, which disabled triple-SP functionality.

Aircraft and weapon compatibility
• Fixed some installed mod aircraft missing from quick-mission and in-mission aircraft selection lists. Paid content remains subject to actual DLC ownership checks.
• Aircraft lists now prefer in-game names and support searches by display name, internal code or ID. Corrected weapon-name references for some mod aircraft while retaining author-provided names.
• Fixed internal SP switching interrupting native hold-button auxiliary functions, improving compatibility with special effects on some mod aircraft.
• Added IRCM mixed-loadout support subject to native compatibility checks, retaining duration, ending and recycling behavior.
• Improved weapon-bay closing after weapon changes on CFA-50, standard Su-57, DarkStar and XFA-33 A/C.

Resupply and management interface
• Fixed a text-replacement error when displaying weapon descriptions during mid-mission resupply, and model-preview fallback when repeatedly switching multi-SP candidates.
• Fixed Backspace and Space in F6 while paused accidentally operating the underlying game menu, retaining normal text editing and key rebinding.
• Enlarged the F6 author watermark and made the feedback card consistent with other cards and fully visible by default. Added a startup update-check option while retaining manual checks.
• Unified log session version labels to prevent conflicting outdated version numbers in the same run.

Installation and author information
• Added a bilingual EXE installer supporting direct installation, updates, uninstallation and universal ZIP export. Improved its home page, language selection and high-DPI display, retaining Vortex and manual installation compatibility.
• Added ReadMe_EN and ReadMe_zhCN请读我 folders to the universal ZIP, each containing four documents in its language. English documents now use English filenames.
• Added author content signatures and distribution-file checks before installation and export; mismatched signatures or files stop the operation. Stripped debug symbols from distribution DLLs without adding per-frame in-game verification.
• Added “Visit my homepage” and “Buy me a coffee” buttons to the installer and F6 author areas, and incorporated the latest feature notes, free-use notice and acknowledgments.

2.25.X
• Fixed internal weapon switching interrupting native hold-button auxiliary effects on some mod aircraft.
• Added main-menu quick mission testing, separate test presets and optional automatic launch on startup.
• Added an installer for one-click download and installation, while retaining Vortex compatibility.

2.24.X
• Added update checks and browser download links, and refined in-game messages.

2.22.X–2.23.X
• Reworked the management interface.
• Improved controller camera controls.
• Added separate model switches for missions and the hangar, and expanded mounting visuals for more aircraft.
• Reduced the overhead of SP weapon switching and interface operations, improving stuttering when switching weapons on the CFA-44.
• Added unlimited ammunition and fast reload options for SP weapons, and expanded support for some third-party aircraft, drones, and gun-type SP weapons.

2.17.X–2.21.X
• Added persistent target tracking, camera hold, and camera recentering controls.
• Added linked fire for the built-in gun, gun pods, and pulse lasers.
• Improved SP weapon switching on aircraft including the CFA-44 and FALKEN.

2.6.X–2.16.X
• Added visuals for carrying multiple SP weapon types on more aircraft, with continued adjustments to mounting points and left/right layouts.
• Improved switching behavior for SP weapons such as drones and lasers, and added the ability to change aircraft and restart the mission.
• Restored the game's original model display for some aircraft with internal weapon bays to reduce stuttering, while retaining multi-SP weapon use.

2.0.X–2.5.X
• Renamed the mod to AC7MultiSP, supporting up to three SP weapon types on one aircraft.
• Added multi-SP weapon model display in the hangar, during resupply, and in missions, plus shortcuts for combinations and models.

1.X and earlier
• Named DualSP, with two SP weapon types, independent ammunition and reloads, and gradual improvements to the management interface and installation methods.
