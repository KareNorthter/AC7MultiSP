AC7MultiSP

2.26.2 beta3 (beta test build)
• Adjusted special weapon launch positions and weapon visibility timing in the bays of the F-22A and standard Su-57.
• Adjusted the opening and closing of the F-22A belly and side bays and improved bay switching on the standard Su-57.

2.26.2 beta2 (beta test build)
• Added multi-SP internal weapon-bay models for the F-22A and adjusted side-bay visibility.
• Adjusted the standard Su-57 dual-4AAM bay layout and switching behavior.

2.26.2 beta (beta test build)
• Added dual-SP and triple-SP models for A-10C and F-2A Super Kai, dual-SP models for F-104C, and an RKT + SAAM dual-SP model for MiG-21bis.
• Added multi-SP internal weapon-bay models for the standard Su-57 and adjusted F-35C bay closing behavior.
• Adjusted the single-MGP pod model for MiG-21bis while retaining dual-stream firing.
• Added SP weapon-data checks and F6 error details. An aircraft error disables enhancements for that aircraft; recovery is attempted after leaving the mission and selecting another aircraft. Severe errors prompt a game restart.
• VR target selection prioritizes the center of the view when it is more than 5° from the aircraft's nose, returning to normal logic within 5°.
• Added continuous horizontal 360° orbiting with camera hold in first-person, third-person and normal cockpit views, and improved right-stick transitions.
• PLSL, MGP and HGP names in the central prompt and lower-right HUD no longer dim during reload or when out of ammunition.
• Corrected the displayed name of native IEWS on CFA-44, ADFX-01 Morgan, XFA-27 and F-2A Super Kai without changing electronic-warfare effects.
• Fixed a possible OSL cleanup error when switching weapons on XFA-36A.
• Hold Start for 3 seconds to open or close the management interface with a controller; error details are no longer repeated at the top of the interface.
• The startup loading notification appears for about three seconds. Update results are shown separately when the check completes; no notification appears when the current version is up to date.

Known issue
• When MiG-21bis uses a single MGP pod, muzzle flashes still appear on both sides when firing. This will be fixed later.

2.26.1
• Improved overall performance, with particular improvements to the CFA-44 and F-2A multi-SP model implementations.
• Fixed the CFA-44 ventral weapon bay failing to close.


2.26.0
• Fixed weapon bays failing to close properly when switching SP weapons on CFA-50, standard Su-57, DarkStar and XFA-33 A/C.
• Added support for the S32's IRCM weapon, allowing it to be carried together with other SP weapons.
• Fixed freezing during mid-mission landing and resupply.

2.25.X
• Fixed the weapon-switch button's hold function not working, supporting ECM and other effects triggered by holding this button on some mod aircraft.
• Added startup logo skipping and quick missions. Select the mission, aircraft, difficulty, skin and 1–3 SP weapons to enter a mission quickly.
• Added an installer for one-click download and installation, while retaining Vortex compatibility.

2.24.X
• Added update checks and browser download links.

2.22.X–2.23.X
• Redesigned the management interface.
• Improved controller camera controls.
• Added separate model switches for missions and the hangar, and expanded mounting visuals for more aircraft.
• Reduced the overhead of SP weapon switching and interface operations, improving stuttering when switching weapons on the CFA-44.
• Added unlimited ammunition and fast reload options for special SP weapons, and expanded support for some third-party aircraft, drones and gun-type SP weapons.

2.17.X–2.21.X
• Added persistent target tracking, camera hold and camera recentering controls.
• Added linked fire for the aircraft gun, gun pods and pulse lasers.

2.6.X–2.16.X
• Added visuals for carrying multiple SP weapon types on more aircraft, with continued adjustments to mounting points and left/right layouts.
• Improved switching behavior for SP weapons such as drones and lasers, and added the ability to change aircraft and restart the mission.
• Restored the game's original model display for some aircraft with internal weapon bays to reduce stuttering, while retaining multi-SP weapon use.

2.0.X–2.5.X
• Renamed the mod to AC7MultiSP, supporting up to three special SP weapon types on one aircraft.
• Added multi-SP weapon model display in the hangar, during resupply and in missions, plus shortcuts for combinations and models.

1.X and earlier
• Named DualSP, with two special SP weapon types, independent ammunition and reloads, and gradual improvements to the management interface and installation methods.
